⚠️ Points of No Return

Before these story moments, stop and complete all missable achievements in the affected areas:

  • Refuge Camp move → all timed quests disappear
  • Ether Mine boss → second half of the mine locks
  • End of Mechonis Field → Sword Valley & Galahad Fortress become inaccessible
  • Agniratha final event → Mechonis Field, Central Factory, Agniratha lock
  • Final point of no return → you cannot leave once entered

The game warns you before each lockout — save in a new slot when you see it.
